# Reading Path

Twelve papers connect evidence about learned world structure to composition, geometry, adaptation, planning, and explicit generalization tests. The four sections can also be read independently. Topic branches below the main path compare additional mechanisms and evaluation protocols.

## 01–03 · Evidence about learned world structure

Physical-variable probes, interventions on learned simulators, and behavioral tests of transferred structure.

### 01 · Interpreting Physics

**[Interpreting Physics in Video World Models](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/joseph26a.html)**

[![ICML: 2026](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICML-2026-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/joseph26a.html) [![arXiv: 2602.07050](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2602.07050-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07050) [![Project: Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Project-Website-2679b5?style=flat-square)](https://www.soniajoseph.ai/interpreting-ph/)

Sonia Joseph et al.

**Method:** Layer-wise linear probes, patch-level decoding, subspace analysis, and attention ablations locate physical information in video encoders. Motion direction forms a circular population code spread over many linear directions, while speed and acceleration become accessible earlier.

**Evaluation:** Probes and targeted steering characterize encoded physical variables and changes in decoded direction. These measurements concern representation geometry; they do not directly measure gains in closed-loop control.

**Connection:** Introduces physical-variable probes and targeted interventions, establishing the kinds of evidence used to study a learned representation.

[Topic: World Model Interpretability](modules/m8-interpretability.md#paper-interpreting-physics)

### 02 · Probing RL World Models

**[What Do World Models Learn in RL? Probing Latent Representations in Learned Environment Simulators](https://openreview.net/forum?id=VE7JJLVKG1)**

[![ICLR: 2026 World Models Workshop](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICLR-2026%20World%20Models%20Workshop-7657a3?style=flat-square)](https://openreview.net/forum?id=VE7JJLVKG1) [![arXiv: 2603.21546](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2603.21546-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21546)

Xinyu Zhang

**Method:** Linear and nonlinear probes recover game-state variables. Hidden-state interventions along probe directions, attention analysis, and token ablations test whether those representations influence generated predictions.

**Evaluation:** Experiments cover IRIS and DIAMOND on Atari Breakout and Pong, measuring game-state decoding and changes in model predictions after hidden-state interventions and token ablations.

**Connection:** Applies probes and interventions to learned environment simulators, linking internal state to generated predictions.

[Topic: World Model Interpretability](modules/m8-interpretability.md#paper-probing-rl-world-models)

### 03 · What Has a Foundation Model Found?

**[What Has a Foundation Model Found? Using Inductive Bias to Probe for World Models](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/vafa25a.html)**

[![ICML: 2025](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICML-2025-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/vafa25a.html) [![arXiv: 2507.06952](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2507.06952-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06952) [![Code: GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-2e8b57?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/keyonvafa/inductive-bias-probes)

Keyon Vafa et al.

**Method:** Inductive-bias probes adapt a foundation model to synthetic tasks generated by a proposed world model. The adaptation behavior tests whether pretraining favors the hypothesized underlying structure.

**Evaluation:** Across several domains, strong training-task prediction can coexist with weak transfer of the proposed structure. Orbital-trajectory models provide a concrete test of transfer to new Newtonian-physics tasks.

**Connection:** Tests proposed world structure through transfer behavior, complementing evidence from internal activations.

[Topic: World Model Interpretability](modules/m8-interpretability.md#paper-what-found)

## 04–06 · Composition and symmetry

Object composition, causal components, and known symmetries as ways to constrain reusable dynamics.

### 04 · HOWM

**[Toward Compositional Generalization in Object-Oriented World Modeling](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/zhao22b.html)**

[![ICML: 2022](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICML-2022-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/zhao22b.html) [![arXiv: 2204.13661](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2204.13661-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.13661) [![Code: GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-2e8b57?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/linfeng-z/HOWM)

Linfeng Zhao et al.

**Method:** An algebraic formulation defines compositional generalization in object-oriented environments. HOWM implements a differentiable, soft form of composition to share dynamics across object configurations.

**Evaluation:** Two instances of the Object Library environment compare methods with exact, approximate, or absent compositional guarantees under a common evaluation pipeline.

**Connection:** Defines object composition as an explicit generalization problem for reusable transition models.

[Topic: Generalization & Adaptation](modules/m3-generalization.md#paper-howm)

### 05 · WM3C

**[Modeling Unseen Environments with Language-guided Composable Causal Components in Reinforcement Learning](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/79d86433c2acd12b6fa98553435d226e-Abstract-Conference.html)**

[![ICLR: 2025](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICLR-2025-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/79d86433c2acd12b6fa98553435d226e-Abstract-Conference.html) [![arXiv: 2505.08361](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2505.08361-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08361)

Xinyue Wang et al.

**Method:** WM3C uses language to organize latent dynamics into composable components. A masked autoencoder, mutual-information constraints, and adaptive sparsity regularization separate transition mechanisms.

**Evaluation:** Numerical simulations and robotic manipulation test latent-process identification, policy learning, and transfer to unseen tasks. Identifiability results depend on the stated modeling assumptions.

**Connection:** Connects compositional mechanisms to language-guided factors and adaptation in unseen environments.

[Topic: Generalization & Adaptation](modules/m3-generalization.md#paper-wm3c)

### 06 · Symmetric Embedding Networks

**[Learning Symmetric Embeddings for Equivariant World Models](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/park22a.html)**

[![ICML: 2022](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICML-2022-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/park22a.html) [![arXiv: 2204.11371](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2204.11371-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11371) [![Code: GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-2e8b57?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/jypark0/sen)

Jung Yeon Park et al.

**Method:** A symmetric embedding network maps observations with a difficult-to-specify group action into features with a prescribed transformation rule. It is trained jointly with an equivariant transition network using a contrastive prediction objective.

**Evaluation:** Transition-prediction experiments compare learned embeddings with fully equivariant and non-equivariant baselines across three forms of symmetry. The latent group action is specified; the learned component connects complex observations to that representation.

**Connection:** Shows how observations can be mapped into features compatible with known symmetries, providing a different source of reusable structure.

[Topic: Representation Geometry & Symmetry](modules/m2-geometry.md#paper-sen)

## 07–09 · Geometry and adaptation

Geometric regularization, inference from context, and representations of changing dynamics.

### 07 · GRWM

**[Cloning Deterministic Worlds: The Critical Role of Latent Geometry in Long-Horizon World Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Xia_Cloning_Deterministic_Worlds_The_Critical_Role_of_Latent_Geometry_in_CVPR_2026_paper.html)**

[![CVPR: 2026](https://img.shields.io/badge/CVPR-2026-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Xia_Cloning_Deterministic_Worlds_The_Critical_Role_of_Latent_Geometry_in_CVPR_2026_paper.html) [![arXiv: 2510.26782](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2510.26782-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26782) [![Code: GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-2e8b57?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/XiaFire/Clone_Deterministic_Environment) [![Project: Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Project-Website-2679b5?style=flat-square)](https://xiafire.github.io/grwm-project-page/)

Zaishuo Xia et al.

**Method:** A temporal-context encoder and geometric regularization based on slowness and uniformity reshape the latent space before dynamics prediction. State-oracle comparisons and component ablations isolate representation quality from predictor capacity.

**Evaluation:** Long-horizon rollouts are evaluated in deterministic environments with fixed rules. The experiments distinguish geometric regularization from the contribution of temporal context.

**Connection:** Relates geometric regularization and temporal context to deterministic rollout accuracy, alongside the explicit symmetry constraints in SEN.

[Topic: Representation Geometry & Symmetry](modules/m2-geometry.md#paper-grwm)

### 08 · Context and Diversity Matter

**[Context and Diversity Matter: The Emergence of In-Context Learning in World Models](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/a46b9dcef23e9160f46d800a49a931d0-Abstract-Conference.html)**

[![ICLR: 2026](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICLR-2026-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/a46b9dcef23e9160f46d800a49a931d0-Abstract-Conference.html) [![arXiv: 2509.22353](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2509.22353-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22353) [![Code: GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-2e8b57?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/airs-cuhk/airsoul/tree/main/projects/MazeWorld)

Fan Wang et al.

**Method:** The analysis separates environment recognition, which identifies a familiar environment, from environment learning, which estimates dynamics from the current context. Error bounds link these mechanisms to context length and training-environment diversity.

**Evaluation:** World-model experiments vary context, data diversity, and architecture to test the theoretical predictions. The distinction concerns how prediction improves with experience while model weights remain fixed.

**Connection:** Examines how interaction history and training diversity support inference in a new environment.

### 09 · Dynamics Are Learned, Not Told

**[Dynamics Are Learned, Not Told: Semi-Supervised Discovery of Latent Dynamics Geometries For Zero-Shot Policy Adaptation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/xu26cq.html)**

[![ICML: 2026](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICML-2026-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/xu26cq.html) [![arXiv: 2606.02280](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2606.02280-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02280) [![Code: GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-2e8b57?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/Mr-Wonderfool/Latent-Dynamics-Geometries)

Zhiming Xu et al.

**Method:** A trajectory encoder learns a dynamics context using a multi-positive InfoNCE objective. The analysis relates encoder smoothness to target-domain regret, motivating a latent geometry that supports adaptation without privileged physical-parameter labels.

**Evaluation:** MuJoCo experiments test severe dynamics shifts, including structural failures outside the explicit parameterization used by identification baselines, alongside in-distribution policy stability.

**Connection:** Uses trajectory-derived context geometry to adapt policies to changed dynamics.

[Topic: Generalization & Adaptation](modules/m3-generalization.md#paper-dynamics-geometries)

## 10–12 · Planning and generalization tests

Latent-space planning, held-out factor combinations, and physical predictions requiring hidden-property inference.

### 10 · DINO-WM

**[DINO-WM: World Models on Pre-trained Visual Features enable Zero-shot Planning](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/zhou25t.html)**

[![ICML: 2025](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICML-2025-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/zhou25t.html) [![arXiv: 2411.04983](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2411.04983-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.04983) [![Code: GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-2e8b57?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/gaoyuezhou/dino_wm) [![Project: Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Project-Website-2679b5?style=flat-square)](https://dino-wm.github.io/)

Gaoyue Zhou et al.

**Method:** A dynamics predictor learns to forecast frozen DINOv2 spatial patch features from offline state-action trajectories. At test time, action sequences are optimized to bring predicted features close to the features of an image goal.

**Evaluation:** Six environments include varied maze layouts, pushing with different object shapes, and multi-particle settings. Goal-directed planning uses neither a learned reward model nor an inverse-dynamics model.

**Connection:** Connects action-conditioned latent prediction to planning and goal-reaching.

[Topic: Planning & Task-Relevant World Models](modules/m4-knowledge-use.md#paper-dino-wm)

### 11 · SVIB

**[Imagine the Unseen World: A Benchmark for Systematic Generalization in Visual World Models](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/58af908d6293810f1a29e69bf723dc48-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html)**

[![NeurIPS: 2023 Datasets & Benchmarks](https://img.shields.io/badge/NeurIPS-2023%20Datasets%20%26%20Benchmarks-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/58af908d6293810f1a29e69bf723dc48-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html) [![arXiv: 2311.09064](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2311.09064-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09064) [![Code: GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-2e8b57?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/systematic-visual-imagination/svib) [![Project: Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Project-Website-2679b5?style=flat-square)](https://systematic-visual-imagination.github.io/)

Yeongbin Kim et al.

**Method:** The Systematic Visual Imagination Benchmark formulates world modeling as one-step image-to-image prediction under latent dynamics. Training splits vary the fraction of factor combinations observed, while different rendering settings vary perceptual difficulty.

**Evaluation:** Held-out combinations test whether models compose familiar factors in new configurations. The protocol isolates systematic visual generalization in one-step predictions; it does not measure long-horizon physical rollouts or closed-loop control.

**Connection:** Makes the generalization split explicit by withholding combinations of familiar factors during training.

### 12 · Physion++

**[Physion++: Evaluating Physical Scene Understanding that Requires Online Inference of Different Physical Properties](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/d3e8011c912e651ab2a76e7935a1e464-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html)**

[![NeurIPS: 2023 Datasets & Benchmarks](https://img.shields.io/badge/NeurIPS-2023%20Datasets%20%26%20Benchmarks-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/d3e8011c912e651ab2a76e7935a1e464-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html) [![arXiv: 2306.15668](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2306.15668-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15668) [![Project: Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Project-Website-2679b5?style=flat-square)](https://dingmyu.github.io/physion_v2/)

Hsiao-Yu Tung et al.

**Method:** Physion++ constructs physical-event prediction problems whose outcomes depend on latent mass, friction, elasticity, or deformability. These properties must be inferred from observed motion and interactions before predicting the event.

**Evaluation:** Physical prediction models with different representations and built-in knowledge are compared with human judgments. Relative to Physion, the added requirement is online inference of hidden physical properties, rather than prediction from visible scene configuration alone.

**Connection:** Tests whether observed interactions support inference of hidden physical properties needed for future-event prediction.
