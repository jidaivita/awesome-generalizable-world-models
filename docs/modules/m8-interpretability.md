# 🔬 World Model Interpretability

Interpretability studies identify the variables represented by a world model and examine their role in prediction. Related model designs give latent variables explicit physical meaning.

Representation probes, interventions on model computation, behavioral tests of learned structure, and physically interpretable architectures provide different forms of evidence.

## Forms of evidence

| Approach | Evidence | Interpretation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Representation probing | A variable can be recovered from activations | The representation contains accessible information |
| Interventions and ablations | Modifying a representation changes predictions | The modified computation influences the measured output |
| Behavioral transfer tests | Adaptation favors or rejects a proposed world structure | Learning behavior reflects a particular inductive bias |
| Interpretable model design | Latent variables are aligned with physical semantics | Interpretability is encouraged through model and supervision choices |

A probe's accuracy alone does not establish that the native predictor or planner uses the decoded variable. Intervention studies specify the output affected and the controls used.

## Representation probing and interventions

<a id="paper-interpreting-physics"></a>
### Interpreting Physics in Video World Models

[![ICML: 2026](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICML-2026-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/joseph26a.html) [![arXiv: 2602.07050](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2602.07050-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07050) [![Project: Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Project-Website-2679b5?style=flat-square)](https://www.soniajoseph.ai/interpreting-ph/)

Sonia Joseph et al.

- **Method:** Layer-wise linear probes, patch-level decoding, subspace analysis, and attention ablations locate physical information in video encoders. Motion direction forms a circular population code spread over many linear directions, while speed and acceleration become accessible earlier.
- **Evaluation:** Probes and targeted steering characterize encoded physical variables and changes in decoded direction. These measurements concern representation geometry; they do not directly measure gains in closed-loop control.
- **Related topics:** [Representation Geometry & Symmetry](m2-geometry.md) · [Generalization & Adaptation](m3-generalization.md)

<a id="paper-probing-rl-world-models"></a>
### What Do World Models Learn in RL? Probing Latent Representations in Learned Environment Simulators

[![ICLR: 2026 World Models Workshop](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICLR-2026%20World%20Models%20Workshop-7657a3?style=flat-square)](https://openreview.net/forum?id=VE7JJLVKG1) [![arXiv: 2603.21546](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2603.21546-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21546)

Xinyu Zhang

- **Method:** Linear and nonlinear probes recover game-state variables. Hidden-state interventions along probe directions, attention analysis, and token ablations test whether those representations influence generated predictions.
- **Evaluation:** Experiments cover IRIS and DIAMOND on Atari Breakout and Pong, measuring game-state decoding and changes in model predictions after hidden-state interventions and token ablations.
- **Related topics:** [Structured & Compositional Representations](m1-structure.md)

## Behavioral tests of learned structure

<a id="paper-what-found"></a>
### What Has a Foundation Model Found? Using Inductive Bias to Probe for World Models

[![ICML: 2025](https://img.shields.io/badge/ICML-2025-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/vafa25a.html) [![arXiv: 2507.06952](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2507.06952-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06952) [![Code: GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-2e8b57?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/keyonvafa/inductive-bias-probes)

Keyon Vafa et al.

- **Method:** Inductive-bias probes adapt a foundation model to synthetic tasks generated by a proposed world model. The adaptation behavior tests whether pretraining favors the hypothesized underlying structure.
- **Evaluation:** Across several domains, strong training-task prediction can coexist with weak transfer of the proposed structure. Orbital-trajectory models provide a concrete test of transfer to new Newtonian-physics tasks.
- **Related topics:** [Generalization & Adaptation](m3-generalization.md)

## Physically interpretable model design

<a id="paper-physically-interpretable"></a>
### Four Principles for Physically Interpretable World Models

[![NeuS: 2025](https://img.shields.io/badge/NeuS-2025-526d82?style=flat-square)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v288/peper25a.html) [![arXiv: 2503.02143](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2503.02143-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02143) [![Code: GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/Code-GitHub-2e8b57?style=flat-square)](https://github.com/Trustworthy-Engineered-Autonomy-Lab/piwm-principles)

Jordan Peper et al.

- **Method:** Four design principles organize latents by physical function, align invariant and equivariant representations, combine supervision strengths, and partition generative outputs for verification.
- **Evaluation:** Experiments on two benchmarks examine the proposed design principles and their contribution to physically meaningful latent representations.
- **Related topics:** [Structured & Compositional Representations](m1-structure.md) · [Representation Geometry & Symmetry](m2-geometry.md)

## Method comparisons

Interpreting Physics analyzes physical information in video representations. What Do World Models Learn in RL? extends probing and interventions to learned Atari simulators. What Has a Foundation Model Found? tests learned structure through adaptation to synthetic tasks, without identifying internal circuits. Four Principles for Physically Interpretable World Models introduces design constraints that align latent variables with physical meaning.
