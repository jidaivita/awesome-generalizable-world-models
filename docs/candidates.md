## JEPA-Bisim: Learning Robust Visual Representations for Planning with Joint-Embedding Predictive World Models

[![arXiv: 2602.18639](https://img.shields.io/badge/arXiv-2602.18639-b31b1b?style=flat-square)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18639)

Leonardo F. Toso et al.

Earlier titles: Learning Invariant Visual Representations for Planning with Joint-Embedding Predictive World Models.

- **Method:** Adds a bisimulation encoder to a joint-embedding predictive world model, placing states with similar transition dynamics near one another while suppressing task-irrelevant visual features.
- **Evaluation:** PointMaze and PushT experiments test planning under background changes and visual distractors with several frozen visual encoders. The reported robustness concerns these observation shifts.
- **Related topic:** [Representation Geometry & Symmetry](modules/m2-geometry.md)
